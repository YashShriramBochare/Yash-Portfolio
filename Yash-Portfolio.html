About Me

Yash Shriram Bochare

My interest in AI started with a simple question: how do you get a system to make a genuinely useful decision under pressure, not just a correct one on paper? That question is basically what Ambu-Link is — routing an ambulance isn't a clean optimization problem, it's a real-time one, with traffic, signals, and time all fighting each other.

At Sanjivani University, I'm in my third semester of an Integrated M.Tech in AI & ML, and most of what I've learned has come from building rather than just studying — Ambu-Link and KuKu (a conversational assistant) are both attempts to take AI/ML theory somewhere it actually has to work. Alongside that, I've picked up entrepreneurship, UX, and cybersecurity fundamentals, and stayed active through hackathons and ambassador programs.

"Build the thing that would actually help someone — not the thing that just satisfies a syllabus."

Everything I build from here on ships under one name — ShriBot-X, the brand I'm building projects and my hackathon work under, starting with Ambu-Link and Bot-X 2.0.
